#### 3.2 Exact Algebraic Branch-Depth Distribution & `branch_hit` Overcount Bug

In `make_prefix_fanout_tree` (`df2_tree.hpp:293-357`):
- Node `1` always has `parent = 0`.
- Node `2` has `parent = 0` (runner-up sibling) **iff `cut == 0`**, and `parent = 1` (primary continuation) **iff `cut >= 1`**.

Therefore, node `2` is reached whenever `(cut == 0)` OR `(cut >= 1 AND node 1 accepted)`. Thus:
$$R_{1,\text{cut}=0} = \text{nr}[2] - \text{na}[1], \qquad R_{1,\text{cut}\ge 1} = \text{tree\_rounds} - \text{nr}[2],$$
where $R_{1,\dots}$ are exact counts of node-1 rejections. Using control's conditional position-0 rejection rate ($\approx 9.5\%$ across `A0` 6.4% and `C1+C2` 9.9%):
- **Run 2 (`T1+T2`, `N = 201`):** $R_{1,\text{cut}=0} = 194 - 125 = 69$ exact, $R_{1,\text{cut}\ge 1} = 201 - 194 = 7$ exact $\implies N_{\text{cut}\ge 1} \approx 7 / 0.095 \approx 74$ (**36.7%**), and **$N_{\text{cut}=0} \approx 127$ (63.3%)**.
  - On `cut >= 1` (`~74` rounds, `~67` passing node 1), primary node 2 accepts at `~91%` $\implies A_{2,\text{cut}\ge 1} \approx 61$.
  - Therefore true **`cut=0` runner-up hits** are $A_{2,\text{cut}=0} = \text{na}[2] - A_{2,\text{cut}\ge 1} \approx 97 - 61 = \mathbf{36}$ out of 127 `cut=0` rounds (**28.3%**, recovering **~52%** of the 69 `cut=0` primary rejections!).
- **Run 1 (`A1`, `N = 1,145`):** $R_{1,\text{cut}=0} = 1091 - 750 = 341$, $R_{1,\text{cut}\ge 1} = 1145 - 1091 = 54 \implies N_{\text{cut}\ge 1} \approx 568$ (**49.6%**), **$N_{\text{cut}=0} \approx 577$ (50.4%)**.
- **`branch_hit` telemetry bug (`df2_tree.hpp:702, 750, 780`):** Currently `if (hit != k || tree.slot_rank[hit] > 0) walk.branch_hit = true;` sets `branch_hit = true` whenever `path[k] != k`. In `PrefixFanout`, the primary tail starts at physical node `cut + fanout + 1` (e.g., node `3` when `cut=0, F=2`), so **every primary-path win past depth `cut + 1` (`slot_rank == 0`) falsely increments `tree_branch_hits`**! That is why `T1+T2` reported `129 / 201 (64.2%)` `tree_branch_hits` even though true `cut=0` runner-up hits were `~36` and total runner-up hits could not exceed `~55`.

### 5. Structural Diagnosis of Low Non-Root Acceptance (~58% vs ~91% Linear)

1. **Mutual-Exclusion Ceiling (`1/F` Cap on Siblings):** At any branch parent with `F` sibling children, at most **one** sibling can match `verifier_argmax`. Thus `sum(accept) / sum(reached)` across `F` reached siblings is mathematically bounded by `1/F` (**`<= 50%` at `F=2`, `<= 33.3%` at `F=3`**), pulling down the pooled non-root conditional rate even if one sibling always hits.
2. **Selection Censoring by `--draft-conf 0.7`:** Linear DF2 only tests rows that cleared `prod p >= 0.7` (yielding `90.8%` conditional acceptance). Trees trigger specifically at the **first uncertain step `cut`** (63% at `cut=0` in `T1+T2`!) where primary confidence has already collapsed, and then spend remaining slots on the unconfident tail behind `cut`.
3. **Caterpillar Leaf-Only Runner-Up Dead-End (`is_caterpillar()`):** Because `PrefixFanout` and `Verifier::run_tree` (`verify.cpp:7272`) restrict runner-up branches to depth-1 leaves (`slot_rank > 0` has no children), winning a runner-up branch yields `+1` draft and immediately stops, discarding all remaining `primary_tail` rows (`nodes 3..7`).
4. **Suboptimal Static Budget Allocation & Sibling Quality (`df2_tree_build_from_selector`, `df2_tree.hpp:362-469`):**
   - **Fixed single-cut + tail flood:** It picks one `cut`, inserts `F` siblings there, and dumps the rest of `M=8` into `primary_tail` (`cut+1..nd-1`), even when `EV(tail[5..7])` is tiny (`0.047–0.104` acc/row) while a runner-up at a *second* uncertain depth (`Caterpillar` shape) has much higher `EV`.
   - **No EV floor / pruning:** Even with `max_fanout=2`, it always emits `M=8` whenever `nd=7`.
   - **Duplicate-token probability leak & prefix collisions:** `seltab_` is computed via an order-1 rank-256 bilinear head over `toptok_[16]` (`design` sec. 7.1) without re-running the 5-layer DF2 backbone. In `df2_tree_build_from_selector`, identical `token_id`s across `toptok[cut][0..15]` are not probability-summed before filtering against `min_branch_p`, and a runner-up matching an already-drafted primary token (or the anchor) is not deprioritized in favor of distinct alternatives.
   - **No adaptive calibration:** The builder trusts raw `dprob` / `seltab` softmax probabilities without tracking actual empirical per-depth acceptance (EWMA).
5. **Telemetry Conflation:** `branch_hit` conflates primary-tail wins with runner-up hits, and `tree_node_reached`/`tree_node_accept` conflate physical node indices across varying `cut` depths.
